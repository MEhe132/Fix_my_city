# Requirement Elicitation Survey: Fix My City

**Introduction:** This survey is designed to gather user requirements for "Fix My City," a proposed web platform that allows citizens to easily report local infrastructure issues (like potholes, broken streetlights, or graffiti) by taking a photo, which automatically extracts location data and alerts local authorities once community support is gathered.
