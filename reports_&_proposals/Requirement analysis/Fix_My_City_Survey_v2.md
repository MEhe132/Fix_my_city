# Requirement Elicitation Survey: Fix My City

**Introduction:** We are building "Fix My City," a platform to report broken public infrastructure (like potholes or streetlights) directly to the local government. To build the best software possible, we need your input on both the technical (system) side and the feature (user) side.

---

## PART 1: System & Technical Requirement Analysis
*(This section helps us understand the technical environment, devices, data privacy, and performance you expect from the software.)*

**1. Which device would you mostly use to report a civic issue while outside?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Smartphone (Android)
- Smartphone (iPhone)
- Tablet
- I would wait until I get home to use a Laptop/Desktop

**2. Would you prefer to download a dedicated Mobile App from the app store, or use a Website directly in your browser?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Dedicated Mobile App (takes up storage space but might be faster)
- Website / Web App (no download required)
- I don't have a preference

**3. When you are out in the city, how reliable is your mobile internet connection?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Very reliable and fast (4G/5G)
- Sometimes slow or spotty
- Poor / I mostly rely on Wi-Fi

**4. If you have no internet connection on the street, would it be useful if the system let you take the photo offline and automatically uploaded it when you reconnect to Wi-Fi?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Yes, absolutely necessary
- Nice to have, but not necessary
- No, I don't need this

**5. How important is it that this system loads very quickly, even on older smartphones?**
*Type: Linear Scale (1-5)*
- 1 = Not important, I have a fast phone

**6. The system needs to know your location to map the issue. Which method do you trust most?**
*Type: Checkboxes*

- Unsure

**8. Do you require any specific accessibility features to comfortably navigate digital applications?**

## PART 2: User & Functional Requirement Analysis
*(This section helps us understand what features you actually need, how you want the platform to work, and how we can keep the community engaged.)*

**9. How often do you notice broken infrastructure (like potholes, graffiti, or broken lights) in your city?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Rarely

**10. "Fix My City" will try to automatically detect the issue's location from your photo. Do you find this feature useful?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Very useful, it saves time
- Neutral
- Not useful, I'd rather type the address

**11. If someone else has already reported the exact same pothole on your street, how should the system handle your new report?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Automatically merge my report with the existing one and add my "Upvote"
- Ask me if I want to add my photo to the existing report
- Keep them as two completely separate reports
- Not sure

**12. To avoid sending too many emails to the government, an issue will only be reported once it gets enough "Upvotes" from the community. What do you think of this?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Good idea, it filters out spam and highlights big problems
- Bad idea, minor issues (with no upvotes) will never get fixed
- Not sure

**13. If an issue you reported remains unresolved by the local council for over 30 days, what automated action would you want the platform to take?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Automatically send a formal Right to Information (RTI) request to the government
- Alert local journalists or media pages
- Escalate the issue to a higher national authority (e.g., 333 helpline)
- Do nothing, just keep it marked as "Pending"

**14. What information would you want to see about an issue reported by someone else?**
*Type: Checkboxes*
- The photo of the issue
- The exact location on a map
- How many upvotes it has
- The current status (e.g., "Pending", "Fixing", "Done")
- Which specific government department is responsible

**15. Would you like the system to send you a notification when an issue you reported (or upvoted) gets fixed?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Yes, via Email
- Yes, via SMS/Text Message
- Yes, inside the app only
- No, I don't need notifications

**16. What feature would most motivate you to consistently report issues over time?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Seeing a public "Leaderboard" of top contributors in my area
- Earning digital badges or a "Civic Hero" status on my profile
- Knowing my reports are sent directly to the local Ward Councillor
- Receiving official feedback directly from government engineers

**17. When reporting a sensitive issue (e.g., illegal dumping or vandalism), would you prefer your report to be completely anonymous?**
*Type: Multiple Choice*
- Yes, completely anonymous to both the public and officials
- Anonymous to the public, but visible to verified government officials
- No, I don't mind my name being attached to my reports

**18. How important is a simple and easy-to-use interface for this application?**

**19. Which language would you prefer the interface to be in?**
